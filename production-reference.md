## Editable PowerPoint layers

reference-exports/slides.json contains eight titles, empty body fields, image paths, timings and transition choices. Text is separate from imagery. Fill empty paragraph fields in PowerPoint as needed; the website intentionally shows minimal finished headlines. No Lorem ipsum, complete speeches or other-team rebuttals appear in the presentation.

presenter-notes.json holds separate speaker/research prompts and source IDs. sources.json and sources.html remain outside the timed slideshow. Add group member assignments and verified evidence in PowerPoint Notes.

## Media and reference images

reference-exports/01.png through 08.png are 1920×1080 final-state visual references. assets contains reusable illustrations, a 1280×720 eight-second mask-removal MP4, and quiet-bell.wav.
