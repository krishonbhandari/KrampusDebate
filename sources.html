  1. A Raucous Christmas: Winter Traditions in Austria
    Austrian National Tourist Office

    Living Austrian winter customs; masks, fur and bells; the association with St. Nicholas; regional cultural identity.

    Tourism interpretation, not a scholarly chronology. The page describes Krampus origins as uncertain. Do not turn speculative ancient origins into established dates.
  2. The Origin of Krampus, Europe's Evil Twist on Santa
    Smithsonian Magazine

    Reported public processions in Lienz; carved wooden masks, fur suits and cowbells.

    Secondary travel reporting. Do not use the article's ancient-origin claims without additional scholarly corroboration. Distinguish Krampus and Perchten rather than treating every regional custom as identical.
  3. Krampus and Perchten
    SalzburgerLand Tourismus GmbH

    Regional costume materials and public processions; official tourism promotion of Krampus events.

    Promotion establishes that events are marketed to visitors, not measured revenue, attendance growth, or a causal economic effect. Event dates on this undated page should not be treated as a current schedule.

Keep claims precise

Injuries and frightened children come from the class scenario. Tourism promotion establishes cultural interest, not a measured economic impact. All presentation images are illustrations, not archival evidence.
